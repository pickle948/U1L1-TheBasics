One Piece has Two main power systems, being Devil Fruits and Haki. Devil fruits are fruits that once eaten, give powers, but at the cost of the user being cursed, being weakened by the ocean and unable to swim. (Sea water is basically Kryptonite.) With enough training, many Devil Fruits can Awaken, giving them more strength and control over their powrer.
  • Devil Fruit Types
    • Zoan
      • Zoan type devil fruits grant the user to transform into an animal or creature, with a full human form, a hybrid form, and a full transformation
      • Zoan fruits will gain increased strength, speed, and other boosts in skill based around their creature once awakened
      • There are three types of Zoans, Normal, Ancient and Mythical
      • Normal zoan fruits allow the user to transform into a creature that you could see in real life
      • Such fruits include:
        • Girrafe
        • Falcon
        • Leopard
      • Ancient zoan fruits let its user turn into an extinct speicies
      • Such fruits include:
        • Spinosaurus
        • Mammoth
        • Saber Tooth Tiger
      • Mythical zoan fruits are possibly the rarest and most powerful fruits in the story, allowing someone to become a mythical being
      • Such fruits include:
        • Pheonix
        • Azure Dragon
        • Buddha
    • Logia
      • Logia Type devil fruits allow its user to control, produce and become an element or substance, becoming intangible after so
      • There are no confirmed logia users who have awakened their fruits, so its unkown exactly what an awakened logia looks like
      • Logia fruits are only able to become vulnurable if attacked with Haki, or has a natural weakness
      • Such fruits include:
        • Flame
        • Sand
        • Lightning
    • Paramecia
      • Paramecia Type Devil fruits are somewhat of a miscellaneous catagory and are the most commonly seen fruit in the story
      • These fruits can give unique superhuman powers, physical change, or the ability to control and produce things
      • A paramecia awakening gives its user the ability to extend its power over the terrain around them, by transforming or altering the area
      • Such fruits include:
        • String
        • Tremor
        • Magnet
    Haki is an energy based on your willpower, and is present in every living creature. It remains weak unless strengthened through training or extreme battles. It divides into three distinct categories, each covering perception, defense, offense, and dominance. Anyone can learn the first two types of Haki with enough training, but the third, you must be born with to harness.
  • Branches of Haki
    • Armament Haki
      • Armament Haki appears as a black and shiny substance that coats your body parts, or an item/weapon to give it more endurance and damage
      • This type of Haki allows you to hit Logia fruit users with ease
      • Advanced Aramament Haki allows someone to have even more defense, and can flow into an object or creature destroying it from the inside out
    • Observation Haki
      • Observation Haki allows someone to sense the presence and emotions of others more easily
      • This can be used to find someone hiding, dodge attacks, and feel the intent of someones actions
      • Future Sight Is a more powerful form of Obseravtion Haki, which allows you to see short glimpses into the future. To use Future Sight, and observation haki in general, you must be calm and in tune with your emotions
    • Conquerors Haki
      • Conquerors Haki is the rarest form of Haki, and 1/1,000,000 people are born with it. There have only been about 30 characters confirmed to have Conquerors Haki, and about 7 of them are confirmed dead. It is also known as The Color of The Supreme King
      • This type of hakis strength is solely based on your willpower, and when expelled, can come in the form of a shockwave. Those whose willpower is weak, will instantly fall unconcious from the blast. It can also take the form of black and red lightning
      • Advanced Conquerors Haki allows users to coat their body and weapons with red and black lightning, greatly increasing their offensive power. (Adveanced Conquerors Haki is a fanmade term, but is a real mechanic)
